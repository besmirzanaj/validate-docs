---
title: "Plans and limits"
description: "How many scans and email checks each plan includes, and when limits reset."
---

Every account has a plan. The plan sets a **daily allowance** of scans and email checks. Allowances reset at **00:00 UTC** each day.

| Plan | Scans per day | Email checks per day |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 | 1, plus 5 welcome checks |
| Enthusiast | 1, plus scan credits | 100 |
| Startup | 10 | 500 |
| Enterprise | 50 | 1,000 |

- **Welcome checks:** every new account gets 5 one-time email checks. They are used only after the day's allowance, and don't expire.
- **Scan credits:** extra scans beyond the daily allowance (Enthusiast plan, or granted by support). They are used only after the day's allowance, and don't expire.
- **What doesn't count:** scans that fail or are cancelled, and email checks that fail on our side (`502`, or the verdict `fatal_exception`). A failed scan paid with a credit gives the credit back.
- To change plan, contact [scan@validate.al](mailto:scan@validate.al).

## When you reach a limit

The API answers `429 Too Many Requests` with a `Retry-After` header (seconds until the reset) and this body:

```json
{
  "detail": {
    "error": "Daily scan limit reached. It resets at 2026-10-05T00:00:00Z.",
    "limit": "scan",
    "resets_at": "2026-10-05T00:00:00Z"
  }
}
```

`limit` is `scan` or `email check`. Wait until `resets_at`, or retry after `Retry-After` seconds.

## See what's left

```bash
curl -H "X-API-Key: your-api-key" https://api.validate.al/me/quota
```

```json
{
  "plan": "free",
  "resets_at": "2026-10-05T00:00:00Z",
  "scans": {"daily_allowance": 1, "used_today": 1, "left_today": 0, "credits": 0},
  "email_checks": {"daily_allowance": 1, "used_today": 1, "left_today": 0, "welcome_left": 4},
  "total_scans": 3,
  "completed_scans": 2
}
```

The [portal](https://portal.validate.al) dashboard shows the same numbers.
